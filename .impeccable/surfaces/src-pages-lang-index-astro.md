## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a hand-coloured atlas of Tucumán. Engraved landscape plates open and close it; between them, the engineering work is drawn as surveyor's plans in the same ink, a business mapped and put in order like a cane estate. It refuses the SaaS landing of saturated colour bands, blueprint grids and same-size cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cream rag paper ground (#F4F1EA body, #FBF6E6 plate paper) and deep green-black ink (#1F2A22). Watercolour washes from the plates: sage for the automation case, straw/harvest ochre for the padel case, lapacho pink and mill-brick as rare accents. Plates are a tone step (plate paper #FBF6E6 on body paper, or a wash field), never ruled frames; captions in serif italic like an atlas; no tracked-caps labels and no monospace except real data (timestamps); no shadows at rest except the floating phone; diagrams in 1-1.5px ink linework with hatching, a compass rose and a scale bar where a plan is shown. The forest-green action button stays.

STORY: The visitor sees Tucumán and a calm promise, then reads how a sale turns into a finished onboarding (the plan of the flow, the live event log beside it), understands the five-step method as the surveyor's process, sees a second estate (the club), learns what their business can do on its own, and writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Unchanged hero minus the phone: "Ordeno tu negocio" over the empty sky, the cane-field plate full bleed across the lower half with its living loop, lead bottom-left, primary action bottom-right, sticky header on paper.

FORM: Atlas of plates and survey plans (user-pinned world; position 1 of 1). seed: none (roll not run: user-pinned world). The roll was not run because the user pinned the world in their own words: "Me gusta muchos los colores (beige) y estetica tranquila que tiene el hero y footer. Quiero poder plasmar eso en el resto de la pagina".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
