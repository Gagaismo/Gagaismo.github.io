The club has two areas, padel and football, a padel school for adults, a programme for kids and a barbecue area that people book. Each part ran on its own: notebooks, messages and spreadsheets.

There was a first prototype, but it covered padel only, kept its data on a single computer and had two kinds of user. There was no football, no stock, no supply requests, no barbecue bookings and no reliable record of who did what. The club needed an online system, used from a phone or a computer, where everyone signs in with their role and one area's data never mixes with another's.
