The academy trains salespeople: the ones who book the calls and the ones who close the sales. Then it connects them with companies looking for people to sell. It sells its courses online, and every sale opens new work: the welcome, access to the course platform, the contract and the instalments.

The owner did all of it by hand, after every payment. The more sales, the more hours went into tasks that repeat the same way every time, and the easier it was to forget something: an email that never went out, a contract nobody chased, an instalment that slipped.
